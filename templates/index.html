Domain Verification

To prevent abuse, you must verify domain ownership by adding this meta tag to your website's <head> section: 

<meta name="breakmysite" content="verified">

  1. Open your site's main HTML template or homepage file.
  2. Find the <head> section near the top.
  3. Paste the meta tag above anywhere inside <head>...</head>.
  4. Save and deploy so the change is live.
  5. Come back here and click Verify Domain Ownership.
